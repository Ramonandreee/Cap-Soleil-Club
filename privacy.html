Privacy

Last updated: [DATA]

This notice explains what happens to the details you leave on the list at Cap Soleil Lawn Tennis Club. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Brazil’s Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD).

Who looks after your data

[RESPONSÁVEL], [ENDEREÇO], is responsible for your data (the “controller”). You can reach us at [EMAIL DE CONTATO].

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name and country, if you choose to give them.
  • Where you found the link (for example “instagram”), taken from the address of the page.
  • That you gave your consent, and when you joined.

We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

For one purpose only: to tell you when the Cap Soleil Pro Shop opens. We do this on the basis of your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a); LGPD article 7, I). We do not sell your details or share them with anyone for their own use.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored in a database run by Supabase in the European Union (Paris, France), protected by encryption and access controls. Only the club can read the list.

To run this page we rely on a small number of providers: [PROVEDORES]. Like any website, they briefly handle technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page. Where a provider is outside the EU or Brazil, it is bound by the safeguards those laws require, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Until we have written to tell you the Pro Shop is open, and in any case for no longer than 12 months after you join. After that, your details are deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your rights

You may ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, have them deleted, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything done before.

To do any of this, write to [EMAIL DE CONTATO] from the address you signed up with. We will reply within 15 days.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority: the ANPD in Brazil, or the supervisory authority where you live in the EU (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
