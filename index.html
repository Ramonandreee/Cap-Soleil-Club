I·The hour

There is an hour, late in the day, when the heat lifts and the shadows reach across the court.

The ball sounds cleaner. The rallies grow longer. Nobody is in a hurry.

Cap Soleil is made for that hour: for playing well, dressing simply and staying on a while after the last point.

II·The grounds

Sun, clay and white.

A court of red clay, swept smooth between sets. A line of shade along the side, and a bench for the next pair. The rest is weather and good company.

    Low and golden over the baseline.

  • Clay
